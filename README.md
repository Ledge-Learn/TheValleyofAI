# The Valley of AI

A crowdsourced archive mapping physical AI installations, billboards, and corporate presence across the Bay Area.

**Live Project:** [thevalleyofai.com](https://thevalleyofai.com)

## Overview

The Valley of AI documents the scale and visibility of AI investment across Silicon Valley. As companies race to establish their AI presence, physical billboards and installations have become markers of corporate commitment to AI. This project captures where companies are investing in visible AI marketing—creating a real-time archive of one of the most significant tech moments in history.

## Features

- **Browse & Explore**: Interactive map and gallery of AI installations across the Bay Area
- **Search & Filter**: Find installations by company, date added, or location
- **Submit Data**: Community-driven contributions allow users to document new sightings
- **Visual Archive**: High-quality images and detailed descriptions of each installation

## Tech Stack

- **Frontend**: HTML, CSS, JavaScript
- **Backend**: Netlify Functions
- **Data**: JSON-based data structure with real-time contributions
- **Hosting**: Netlify
- **AI Tools**: Claude (research, data organization, content generation)

## How It Was Built

This project leverages AI at every stage:

1. **Research & Data Collection**: Used Claude to help research and organize initial Bay Area AI installations and corporate presence data
2. **Content Generation**: Claude assisted in writing descriptions, analyzing trends, and contextualizing installations
3. **Development**: Used Claude for code development, architecture decisions, and problem-solving
4. **Data Organization**: Structured crowdsourced data using AI-assisted validation and formatting

The project combines curated data (researched by me and contributors) with real examples submitted by the community, creating a dynamic, growing archive.

## How to Use

1. Visit [thevalleyofai.com](https://thevalleyofai.com)
2. Browse the map or gallery view to explore installations
3. Search by company name or location
4. Submit a new installation or correction via the contribution form

## Getting Involved

Have you spotted an AI billboard or installation in the Bay Area? Submit it to help document this moment in tech history.

**Contribute**: Click "Submit" on the website to add new installations with photos and details.

## Why This Matters

AI infrastructure is no longer invisible. Companies are signaling their AI commitment through visible, physical presence. The Valley of AI creates a tangible record of where major technology investments are happening—useful for researchers, business analysts, students, and anyone interested in understanding the scope and geography of the AI boom.
